# The control brief (example)

Copy to `control-brief.md` (or `control-brief.local.md`, which is gitignored) and write your own.
This is the untailored self-introduction. Use it verbatim when there is no link to read, when the
recipient's role is unknown, or as the baseline a tailored message has to beat. Every claim here
must also appear in `facts.md`.

## One line (DM openers, bios)

`<I'm NAME, ROLE, and I DO WHAT, for WHOM.>`

## Standard (cold email, three sentences)

`<Sentence 1: who you are and what you are building or doing now.>`
`<Sentence 2: the credential, with one verified number.>`
`<Sentence 3: the problem you care about, stated plainly.>`

## Long (partnerships, press, advisory)

`<Two short paragraphs: where you came from, with figures from facts.md; what you are doing now
and why it matters to the reader.>`

## Registers

- **Peer:** shortest form, first names, one concrete detail.
- **Formal:** full name and title, the standard version, a specific ask.
