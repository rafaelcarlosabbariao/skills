The deck is shallow; the preparation is deep. One idea per slide, and behind every claim on a slide there must be three layers to descend through under questioning — the claim, the reasoning, the evidence with its sample size. Anything that cannot be spoken to for ten minutes does not go on a slide.
reading the marks

Espresso panels like this one are asides to myself, not slide copy. Dashed rules mark material held under the slide — prepared, not presented, spoken only if a question reaches for it. Everything on a cream plate is a figure or a table and is queryable.
