# Cheatsheet: gossip-reputation shelf

Decision tables and quick rules. Open this first under time pressure; each row points
to the chapter or pattern with the full mechanics.

## Master fork: what is this talk doing

| Situation | Which lens | What to check first |
|---|---|---|
| A community gossips intensely about a member | Membership and maintenance (gossip-and-scandal-gluckman) | The alleged norm breach, and whether tellers rotate |
| A leak, expose, or insider account surfaces | Information management (what-is-gossip-about-paine) | Who benefits; the first-teller and their stake |
| A scandal wave with a personal target | The unsaid read (patterns 4) | Which value conflict is wearing the person as costume |
| Cooperation or quality floor collapsing | Sensor-effector audit (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg) | Which half broke: the talk channel or the consequence |
| A cancellation or ban wave | Effector arm reading (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg) | Whether re-entry exists: reform system or removal system |
| A corpus full of who-did-what talk | Capacity baseline (grooming-gossip-dunbar) | Two-thirds social talk is normal; deviation is the finding |
| Community rejects AI content violently | Stake check (patterns 5) | What the speaker stakes; nothing staked means outside the mechanism |
| Talk about a member stops entirely | Expulsion in progress (gossip-and-scandal-gluckman) | Whether gossip rights were withdrawn |

## The Gluckman-Paine fork

| Evidence | Reading |
|---|---|
| Tellers rotate; talk rehearses a shared norm; no teller visibly profits | Gluckman: maintenance. The group is doing upkeep |
| Same actors originate; first-teller's standing improves; rivals damaged | Paine: campaign. The talk is a move |
| Both present | Report the mix; most real corpora carry both, and the ratio is the finding |

## Scale rules

| Observation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Active core under ~150 | Peer knowledge can enforce norms; expect informal governance |
| Active core crossing ~150 | Expect formal moderation to appear and to be resented; a phase change, not a failure |
| Wave faster than conversation could carry | Pushed, not told; check the algorithm before crediting word of mouth |
| Low visible enforcement, high cooperation | Deterrence working, not absence of mechanism |

## Standing cautions

- The corpus records tellings, not events. Read speakers before topics.
- Dunbar's numbers are 1990s British samples; Feinberg's are lab-scale. Carry
  mechanisms and directions, re-measure magnitudes locally.
- Being gossiped about inside a group is belonging; the exclusion is being denied
  the talk.
- Quiet channels still work; scandal-free is not tension-free (check the unsaid).
- Instrument map: THE UNSAID reads Gluckman, WHO'S TALKING reads Paine, WORD reads
  Dunbar, and Feinberg underwrites the acronym's claim itself.
