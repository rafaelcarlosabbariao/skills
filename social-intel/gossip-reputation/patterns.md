# Patterns: gossip-reputation shelf

Reusable recipes assembled across the four works. Each pattern names when to use it,
the steps, and its source works. Run the teller pre-pass first; every other pattern
depends on knowing who is speaking.

## 1. The teller pre-pass

**When to use:** before reading any gossip event, scandal wave, leak, or discourse
spike. The corpus records tellings, not events.

**Steps:**
1. Identify the first-teller of the account, not just its loudest relayers.
2. Code the teller's stake: what did the telling earn them (standing, alliance,
   damage to a rival, nothing visible)?
3. Compute the originator-to-relayer ratio for the wave. Same actors always
   originating means a managed flow.
4. Route: teller profits and originators concentrate, read Paine (a campaign);
   tellers rotate and the talk rehearses a shared norm, read Gluckman (maintenance).
   Both present, report the mix.

**Source works:** what-is-gossip-about-paine, gossip-and-scandal-gluckman.

## 2. The boundary read

**When to use:** mapping a community's real membership, for an audience simulation or for reading
who a scandal belongs to.

**Steps:**
1. Map who gossips about whom, and who is permitted to join which talk.
2. Draw the boundary at the edge of gossip rights, not at the stated membership.
3. Flag members no longer gossiped about: expulsion in progress.
4. Check scale: an active core near or above ~150 predicts a shift from
   peer-enforced norms to formal moderation, and the friction of that shift.

**Source works:** gossip-and-scandal-gluckman, grooming-gossip-dunbar.

## 3. The sensor-effector audit

**When to use:** diagnosing why a community's cooperation, quality floor, or norm
enforcement is rising or collapsing.

**Steps:**
1. Locate the sensor: where does peer talk about members' conduct actually happen
   (group chat, comments, backchannel)? Absent or suppressed means no sensor.
2. Locate the effector: what consequence attaches to a bad reputation (exclusion,
   demotion, silence)? None means no effector.
3. Check the pairing: sensor without effector predicts cynicism (everyone knows,
   nothing happens); effector without sensor predicts arbitrary enforcement.
4. Check re-entry: exclusion with a path back reforms; without one it only removes.
   Read cancellation debates as arguments about re-entry design.
5. Count the quiet: deterrence works invisibly, so low visible enforcement with high
   cooperation is the mechanism succeeding, not absent.

**Source works:** gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg, gossip-and-scandal-gluckman.

## 4. The unsaid read

**When to use:** scoring cultural tension; finding what a group cannot say
officially.

**Steps:**
1. Find where the group's informal talk pools: the venues where members judge
   conduct off the record.
2. List what is judged there but never stated in the group's official voice; that
   list is the unsaid.
3. Watch for personalization: a value conflict surfacing as scandal about a person
   (a performative male, an exfluencer, an AI-music defector) is the tension
   finding a costume.
4. Score escalation by the gap: the wider the distance between official voice and
   informal judgment, the higher the tension reading.

**Source works:** gossip-and-scandal-gluckman, what-is-gossip-about-paine.

## 5. The stake check (slop and synthetic speech)

**When to use:** reading a community's reaction to machine-generated content, or
judging whether a speech wave can be disciplined by reputation at all.

**Steps:**
1. Ask what the speaker stakes on the telling. A human gossip stakes their
   credibility; accountable speech is inside the mechanism.
2. Machine output, anonymous flooding, and unattributable waves stake nothing:
   outside the mechanism, immune to ostracism, and predictably read as pollution by
   communities that run on reputation.
3. Read demands for provenance marks, human-made labels, and proof-of-personhood as
   the mechanism demanding its precondition (identifiable, excludable actors) back.
4. For velocity reads, remember capacity: word of mouth is bounded by conversation
   group size and time budgets; a wave faster than talk can carry was pushed, not
   told.

**Source works:** grooming-gossip-dunbar, gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg.
