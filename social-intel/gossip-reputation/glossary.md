# Glossary: gossip-reputation shelf

Merged terms from all five chapters, alphabetical. Each entry names the chapter that
defines it.

**Deterrence by reputation** (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg): behavior
changed by the possibility of being talked about and excluded, before any talk
occurs; the reason a quiet enforcement channel is not an idle one.

**Dunbar's number** (grooming-gossip-dunbar): roughly 150, the group size at which
each member can still hold a working social model of every other; the ceiling of
peer-enforced norms.

**First-teller** (what-is-gossip-about-paine): the originator of an account, whose
identity and payoff discriminate between the Gluckman and Paine readings.

**Free rider** (grooming-gossip-dunbar, gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg): a
member taking group benefits without paying group costs; the figure gossip exists to
expose and ostracism to discipline.

**Gossip-ostracism pairing** (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg): the sensor and
effector of a reputation system; talk identifies, exclusion enforces, and either
alone underperforms.

**Grooming at a distance** (grooming-gossip-dunbar): conversation as social grooming
that services several relationships at once, breaking the one-at-a-time budget.

**Information broker** (what-is-gossip-about-paine): an actor whose standing rests on
controlling flows between parts of a network that do not talk directly.

**Information management** (what-is-gossip-about-paine): the self-interested control
of what circulates about oneself and others; Paine's core mechanism.

**Information validation** (gossip-motives-hartung): gossiping to check one's existing
view of the absent person against the listener's; the motive people ranked first.

**Membership right** (gossip-and-scandal-gluckman): the privilege of joining the talk
about a group's members; who may gossip about whom maps the group's real boundary.

**Moral community** (gossip-and-scandal-gluckman): the circle within which members
may judge each other's conduct by shared values, enacted through gossip.

**Negative influence** (gossip-motives-hartung): gossiping to damage the absent person's
reputation; the motive people ranked last.

**Ostracism** (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg): exclusion from group
participation; the effector arm, whose re-entry design decides reform versus
removal.

**Protection motive** (gossip-motives-hartung): gossiping to warn the listener about the
absent person; read as group protection over time.

**Public-goods game** (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg): an experimental design
where private contributions benefit the group, making free riding individually
tempting and collectively costly.

**Reputational information** (gossip-ostracism-cooperation-feinberg): peer-passed
accounts of a member's past conduct, the currency the mechanism runs on.

**Scandal** (gossip-and-scandal-gluckman): gossip escalated to public evaluation,
through which status competition and boundary enforcement are conducted.

**Social brain hypothesis** (grooming-gossip-dunbar): the claim that primate brain
expansion is driven by the demands of social modeling rather than ecology.

**Social grooming** (grooming-gossip-dunbar): time-costly one-to-one maintenance of
alliances in primates, which language partially replaces in humans.

**Transactionalism** (what-is-gossip-about-paine): reading social behavior as
exchanges between self-interested actors rather than as expressions of group
function.

**Unity maintenance** (gossip-and-scandal-gluckman): the daily informal work of
keeping a group coherent, which Gluckman assigns to gossip rather than to
institutions.
